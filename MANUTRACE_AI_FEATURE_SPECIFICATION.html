📐 5. Mathematical SPC Formulas & Empirical Models

SPC Z-Score Anomaly Formula:
Z = |Tcurrent - Tbaseline| / σ
(Where Tbaseline = 180.0°C and σ = 2.5°C. Anomaly triggered when Z > 2.0 or |T - 180| > 5°C)
Multi-Variate Composite Risk Rating:
Riskcomposite = 0.40 × Rthermal + 0.25 × Rvibration + 0.20 × Rsupplier + 0.15 × Rvariance
What-If Process Simulation Response Functions:
ΔQualityRisk = 2.4 × ΔT + 3.1 × ΔP - 0.8 × Δt
ΔDefectRisk = 1.6 × ΔT + 1.2 × (ΔSpeed / 100) + 1.8 × ΔP
